# MCP Hub - Financial Projections & Unit Economics

## Executive Summary

MCP Hub's financial model demonstrates strong unit economics with a path to $85M+ ARR by Year 5. The subscription-based business model achieves 65% gross margins with healthy LTV/CAC ratios across all customer segments, positioning the company for sustainable growth and attractive investor returns.

**Key Financial Highlights:**
- **Strong Unit Economics**: LTV/CAC ratios of 9:1 to 45:1 across segments
- **Predictable Revenue**: 95%+ recurring subscription revenue model
- **High Margins**: 65% gross margins by Year 3, improving to 72% by Year 5
- **Path to Profitability**: EBITDA positive by Month 18, cash flow positive by Month 24

## Unit Economics & Key Metrics

### Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC)

**Enterprise Segment**
- **Sales & Marketing Cost**: $1,200 per customer
- **Payback Period**: 18 months
- **Calculation**: (Sales team cost + Marketing allocation) / New customers acquired

**Professional Segment**
- **Sales & Marketing Cost**: $180 per customer
- **Payback Period**: 12 months
- **Calculation**: (Performance marketing + content) / New signups

**Services Segment**
- **Sales & Marketing Cost**: $500 per engagement
- **Payback Period**: Immediate (upfront payment)
- **Calculation**: (Business development + partner support) / New certifications

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Enterprise Customers**
- **Average Revenue per User (ARPU)**: $60,000 annually
- **Gross Margin**: 72% (Year 3+)
- **Average Customer Lifespan**: 4.5 years
- **Churn Rate**: 5% annually (enterprise stickiness)
- **LTV Calculation**: $60,000 × 0.72 × 4.5 = $194,400

**Professional Customers**
- **Average Revenue per User (ARPU)**: $1,200 annually
- **Gross Margin**: 68% (Year 3+)
- **Average Customer Lifespan**: 3 years
- **Churn Rate**: 12% annually
- **LTV Calculation**: $1,200 × 0.68 × 3 = $2,448

**Services Customers**
- **Average Engagement Value**: $12,500
- **Gross Margin**: 75%
- **Repeat Rate**: 60% (annual recertification)
- **LTV Calculation**: $12,500 × 0.75 × 2.5 = $23,438

### LTV/CAC Ratios

| Segment | LTV | CAC | LTV/CAC Ratio | Payback Period |
|---------|-----|-----|---------------|----------------|
| Enterprise | $194,400 | $1,200 | **162:1** | 18 months |
| Professional | $2,448 | $180 | **14:1** | 12 months |
| Services | $23,438 | $500 | **47:1** | Immediate |

**Industry Benchmarks**: SaaS companies typically target 3:1 LTV/CAC ratios. MCP Hub's ratios significantly exceed industry standards, indicating strong unit economics.
